A yard, a short list, and a straight answer.

Brimotors Limited is a family-run motor vehicle dealership on Kiambu Road, Nairobi. We import directly, buy locally, and sell fewer cars than we could — on purpose.

Who we are

We sell the cars we’d put our own families in.

Kenya’s used-car market rewards volume: buy cheap at auction, mark it up, move it on. We took the slower route. Our stock is deliberately short — currently seventeen vehicles — because every car gets inspected, priced honestly and described with the numbers that matter.

Most of what we sell is imported directly from Japan, the UK, Thailand and South Africa. The rest is local: clean cars we buy outright or take in trade, usually from customers who are buying their next one from us.

You’ll find us 50 metres from the Northern Bypass, just past Fourways Junction. Park in the yard, take your time, and drive the car before you decide.

How we work

Four rules we don’t bend.

They cost us a sale now and then. They also mean most of our customers come back, and send their friends.

  • 01

    Condition before price

    We walk away from cheap units with hidden accident repair or a questionable odometer. There is always another car.

  • 02

    Numbers on the listing

    Mileage, engine size, gearbox, fuel and colour are published. No “ask for details” on the basics.

  • 03

    The price is the price

    One asking price, agreed before you travel. No surprise “yard fees” at signing, no bait listings.

  • 04

    We’ll tell you when to walk

    If the car you came for isn’t right, we’ll say so — and show you what is, even if it’s cheaper.
