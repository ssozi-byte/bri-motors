Before you come

Two things that save a wasted trip.

Call about the specific car first

Stock moves quickly — a car listed on Monday can be gone by Thursday. A quick call confirms it’s still on the yard and lets us have it cleaned and out front.

Bring your licence and ID

You need a valid licence for a test drive, and your ID for any purchase or trade-in paperwork. For financing, bring your payslips, bank statements and KRA PIN.
