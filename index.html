01 — Who we are

A small yard on Kiambu Road. A short list of cars we’d buy ourselves.

More about Brimotors

We are a motor vehicle dealership on Kiambu Road, just past Fourways Junction and 50 metres from the Northern Bypass. Most of what we sell arrives directly from Japan, the UK, Thailand and South Africa; the rest are clean local cars we know the history of.

That means fewer cars on the lot and a lot more said about each one. Every listing carries the mileage, engine size, transmission and fuel type we verified — no guessing, no vague “excellent condition”. If a car isn’t right for you, we’ll tell you what is.

03 — How we buy

Sourced properly, or not at all.

A cheap car is only cheap until the first repair. We buy on condition and paperwork first, price second — and we show you what we found before you commit.

The full import process

01 / Brief

Tell us the job the car has to do

School run, farm, upcountry weekends or a first car. Model, budget, fuel and gearbox — we’ll shortlist what actually fits.

02 / Inspect

We check it before money moves

Mileage, body, engine, service records and accident history where available. Photos and a written summary come to you.

03 / Hand over

Cleared, plated, and ready to drive

Shipping, duty and clearing handled on imports. You collect from Kiambu Road, or we arrange delivery within Nairobi.
