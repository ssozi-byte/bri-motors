We buy from four markets and sell into one. Whether the car already exists or you want us to find it, the process is documented and the price is agreed before anything moves.

The import process

Four steps, no surprises.

Imports take weeks, not days. What we control is how much you know at each stage — and we put the timeline in writing.

01

Brief

Model, year range, budget, fuel and gearbox. We agree a landed-cost range before we bid on anything.

02

Source & inspect

Auction sheets, inspections and photos from the source market. You approve the unit before it is bought.

03

Ship & clear

Freight, duty, clearing and registration handled by us. You get documents at each handover point.

04

Prep & keys

Valet, final check and a test drive at the yard on Kiambu Road before you sign anything.

03 / Sell or trade

We buy clean local cars — outright or as trade

Bring the car, the logbook and your ID to Kiambu Road. We inspect it, tell you what it needs, and make an offer the same day. If you’re trading, the value comes straight off the price of the car you’re buying.

  • Free, same-day valuation
  • Cash purchase or trade-in
  • We also sell on behalf of owners
  • No obligation to accept the offer

04 / Financing

Cash or bank financing — we handle the file

Most banks will lend against a clean, locally registered vehicle. We prepare the invoice and vehicle documents, help assemble your application, and follow it through with you. You choose the lender; we don’t add a markup to the loan.

  • Deposit from 20%, term up to 5 years
  • Trade-in can cover the deposit
  • Vehicle documents prepared for the bank
  • 48-hour hold while approval runs

Questions

The things people ask before they visit.

Anything else, call the yard on 0722 114 035 during opening hours.

Can I inspect a car before paying?

Always. Come to Kiambu Road during opening hours, bring your licence and drive it. We encourage a third-party inspection if you want one — we’ll make the car available for it.

Do you take trade-ins?

Yes. We buy clean local cars outright and accept trade-ins against stock. Bring the logbook and ID for a same-day valuation.

Can you finance the car for me?

We don’t lend directly; we prepare the file and work with banks. Deposits typically start at 20% with terms up to five years, and the final rate is set by the lender.

How long does a special import take?

It depends on the source market and shipping schedule. We give you a written timeline with the landed-cost quote before you commit, and update you at each stage.

Are the mileages on the listings verified?

Each listing shows the mileage recorded for that vehicle. For imports we work from the source-market inspection and auction sheet; for local cars we inspect and record the odometer at intake.

Do you sell on behalf of owners?

Yes, on agreed terms. Send photos, mileage and your asking price on WhatsApp and we’ll tell you whether we can take it on.

Start with a brief, not a deposit.

Tell us the car and the budget. We’ll come back with what’s possible, what it lands at, and how long it takes.
